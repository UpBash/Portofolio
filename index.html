About Me

Professional Overview

I'm Panos—a DataOps Engineer and AI Researcher passionate about solving complex data challenges through innovative technical approaches. Currently at EnablerMinds (Frankfurt, Germany) as a DataOps Engineer, I architect and maintain enterprise-scale data solutions combining cloud infrastructure, analytics engineering, and collaborative development practices.

DataOps & Data Engineering Expertise

My current role focuses on building reliable, scalable data pipelines and platforms across the modern data stack:

  • Cloud Data Integration: Azure Data Factory (ADF) pipeline design, maintenance, and troubleshooting. I work with datasets, linked services, activities, and parameterized configurations to orchestrate data ingestion, movement, and processing workflows across environments.
  • Data Platforms & Orchestration: Databricks expertise including job configuration, task scheduling, dependency management, and notebook-based processing. I maintain Unity Catalog implementations for governed data asset organization and implement configuration-driven approaches that separate reusable logic from dataset-specific metadata.
  • Analytics Engineering: dbt development for modular data transformation models. I design sources, models, and validation checks that improve data reliability and maintainability across the analytics layer. Strong focus on model dependencies, documentation, and collaborative transformation patterns.
  • CI/CD & Infrastructure: Azure Pipelines configuration for automated testing and deployment of data infrastructure changes. I coordinate multi-repository deployments and maintain consistency between code, configurations, and infrastructure requirements.
  • Cross-Team Collaboration: Experience supporting multiple teams and assignments through Jira, translating business requirements into pipeline logic, job definitions, and configuration changes. I document solutions and maintain operational knowledge for sustainable team handovers.

Career Journey

My professional journey combines hands-on data engineering execution with cutting-edge AI research. I started at the National Observatory of Athens analyzing solar energy data with time series forecasting, then moved into medical research at NKUA developing machine learning models for classification tasks. This foundation evolved into specialized data engineering roles at Tgndata and Accenture, where I designed end-to-end ETL pipelines and analytics platforms for enterprise clients. My current role at EnablerMinds represents the synthesis of these experiences—applying full-stack data engineering practices to build maintainable, enterprise-scale data solutions.

My academic background includes a Master's thesis on deterministic and stochastic partial differential equations solved using advanced machine learning techniques— a unique blend of mathematical rigor and modern AI methodologies that informs my approach to complex data engineering challenges.

Approach & Philosophy

I'm driven by continuous learning and mastery of emerging technologies. My approach emphasizes:

  • Reliability & Scalability: Building data solutions that are robust, testable, and can grow with organizational needs.
  • Maintainability: Writing code and configurations that are clear, well-documented, and follow established conventions, enabling sustainable team collaboration.
  • Collaboration: Translating between technical and business contexts, supporting multiple teams, and documenting decisions for knowledge transfer.
  • Operational Excellence: Understanding not just the "what" and "why" but also the "how" of running data systems reliably in production.
  • Continuous Improvement: Investigating failures, identifying root causes, and implementing targeted improvements that reduce future incidents and enhance system efficiency.

My ultimate goal is to contribute meaningfully to the data engineering and AI fields by turning complex data into actionable insights, while building systems and practices that enable teams to operate at scale with confidence and clarity.
